About Sesco Tyres

Who we are

100% Certified
Master Fitment Bay Kampala Workshop

Authorized Dealer of Global Tyre Brand "Rodeo" in Uganda

SESCO TYRES is a locally owned and registered company specializing in the importation, distribution, selling of high quality and world class tyres, tubes, flaps and other tyre accessories like tyre repair seals, valves, tyre O rings, tyre repair kits, motor vehicle rims and rim rings of all sizes. The company is reputed to offer high quality services in tyres and aftersales services, tyre Fitting, wheel balancing, HD wheel alignment using the latest tools, machines and technology at our offices at Queen’s way. Our brands are sourced from different manufacturers in different countries which include Thailand, Korea and China and our product stocks include brands like, Rodeo, Roadstone and Otani and in all the above brands we are the sole registered agents to distribute and supply the said brands with manufacturers' authorizations and after sales warranties.

  • 100% Genuine, fresh-batch factory tyres with full warranty

Ordinary Informal Workshops

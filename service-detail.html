Basic Car Servicing

An oil change is the most important part of any car service.

An oil change is the most important part of any car service. If oil is not changed regularly, sludge can build up and will stop the engine from being sufficiently lubricated. The engine can be thought of as the heart of the car, and if not lubricated, engine failure is likely and repairs are expensive.

Coolant is also critical as it degrades over time and loses its ability to inhibit corrosion inside the engine as well as its cooling efficiency. Failing to service your vehicle to save a little really is a bad decision. Regular servicing can help to identify issues early on that could later become expensive to rectify.

Oil filters for cars are an important component to keep your engine working properly. Oil filters are vital to maintain the quality of the oil that functions to lubricate the engine, catching microscopic dirt, metal shavings, and carbon particles before they can score internal cylinder walls.

Why Our Basic Car Servicing?

Regularly scheduled oil changes are a must. Hands down, they are not a vehicle service you can ignore. Old, dirty oil decreases gas mileage, lowers horsepower, and will ultimately reduce the operating life of your engine.

  • Regular Oil Changes Keep Your Engine Clean
  • Longer Engine Life with Routine Maintenance
  • Changing Oil Protects Other Engine Parts
  • Better Gas Mileage by Changing Oil On Time
  • Pass Vehicle Emissions Tests & Protect Environment
  • Routine Changes Deliver Better Performance

03 / Precision Balancing

Tyre Balancing

Wheel balancing is the process of matching the weight of the combined tyre and wheel assembly.

Wheel balancing is the process of matching the weight of the combined tyre and wheel assembly so that it spins smoothly at high speed. Balancing involves putting the wheel/tyre on a calibrated spin balancer, which centers the wheel and spins it to determine exactly where counter-weights must go.

At high speeds, a tiny imbalance in weight is intensified by centrifugal force, causing the wheel/tyre to spin with an irregular, clumping motion. This usually translates into a vibration in the car that is not only uncomfortable but also results in premature wearing of suspension and steering components, rotating parts, and tyres.

The first sign that your wheels may be out of balance is when your steering wheel starts to wobble above a certain speed. A driver may not always sense an imbalance at the steering wheel; it could be dampened by vehicle weight. This is why balancing is equally important for both front and rear wheels and is recommended each time a tyre is removed or replaced.

Why Our Tyre Balancing?

When you have tyre balancing service done at Sesco Tyres, our certified technicians use computerized dynamic spin balancers to test both static and dynamic balance. Modern tyre design is more responsive, with lower profiles for style and performance. Correct balance is critical to prevent tyre cupping and suspension wear.

  • Eliminates Steering Wheel Wobble & Cabin Vibration
  • Prevents Premature & Irregular Tread Wear
  • Extends Life of Suspension & Steering Bushings
  • Recommended Every 5,000–10,000 km or After Tyre Change

Tyre Fitting

An incorrectly fitted tyre can become damaged and burst, leading to serious safety hazards.

An incorrectly fitted tyre can become damaged and burst, and could lead to traffic accidents involving vehicle damage and serious personal injury. That's why it's essential to have tyres fitted by an experienced technician who will take the necessary precautions and use only approved fitting tools and lubricants.

Stony tracks, uneven roads, potholes — tyres in Uganda have to withstand a lot. As the direct link between the car and the road, they are subject to a lot of stress. They must be replaced from time to time to ensure that they always roll reliably. This means that the tyre rubber must be demounted from the rim cleanly and replaced without damage to the bead bundle.

Unlike changing a spare wheel on the roadside, you should never attempt to mount tyres on rims on your own. It is best to have tyre changes carried out by our specialists using special tyre-mounting machines that avoid pinching the inner liner or scratching expensive alloy rims.

05 / Tread Longevity

Tyre Rotation

Tyres are expensive, so you want to make them last as long as possible.

Tyres are expensive, so you want to make them last as long as possible. It is incredibly frustrating to replace tyres that are only worn out on one side or only on the front axle. To avoid premature replacement, you need to rotate your tyres periodically. If one tyre looks more worn than the other, it's time to shift these tyres around to other positions on the car.

No matter what kind of car you drive, the tyres at each corner are all doing a slightly different job, which means they wear differently. Front-wheel-drive cars in particular make their front tyres work much harder than the ones in the back. The front pair bears all the burden of accelerating and turning as well as most of the forces of braking.

You're not off the hook if you drive a rear- or all-wheel-drive vehicle, either. Any car will wear its tyres in different ways just because of the peculiarities of that car's suspension or weight distribution. Regular rotation ensures that tread depth wears down uniformly, preserving responsive steering and stopping grip.

Why Our Tyre Rotation?

Most vehicle manufacturers recommend tyre rotation every 8,000 to 10,000 km. When you bring your car to Sesco Tyres for rotation, it's also an ideal time to have the tyres inspected for punctures, irregular tread depth, and air pressure.

  • Promotes Balanced Handling & Wet Road Grip
  • Correct Rotational Patterns (Directional & Asymmetric)
  • Multi-Point Tread & Inflation Inspection Included
Recommended IntervalEvery 8,000 – 10,000 km

Wheel Alignment

At Sesco tyres, we believe one of the most important considerations to vehicle maintenance is to ensure your wheels are correctly aligned.

At Sesco tyres, we believe one of the most important considerations to vehicle maintenance is to ensure your wheels are correctly aligned.

Wheel alignment means adjusting the angles of the wheels so that they are set exactly to the manufacturer's specification. This ensures wheels lie perpendicular to the ground and parallel to each other. The concept might seem straightforward, but it requires millimeter-level precision using optical sensors and laser targets.

While you're behind the wheel, you want the steering and wheels to be in complete harmony under your command. Proper alignment ensures your car travels dead straight without pulling to the left or right, and returns smoothly to center after turns.

Why Our Wheel Alignment?

Maintaining proper wheel alignment is essential to avoid unnecessary wear on your tyres, steering rack, suspension bushings, and brakes. Accurate 3D computerized wheel alignment optimises driving stability, maximises tyre life, and improves your vehicle's overall fuel economy.
